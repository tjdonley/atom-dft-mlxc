This is structural, not a tuning miss: at a reference node the kernel adds DELTA_new and the

backbone only matters BETWEEN nodes / out of domain. The 88 mHa is the exact-hole-REPRODUCTION
residual: R_c=6 window leakage on the references + kernel interpolation under sparse FPS nodes. It is
also robust to kernel bandwidth (l0=0.7 -> 83, l0=0.3 -> 100 on a 6-atom subset) -- bandwidth shifts
it but cannot push it below PBE.

VERDICT: the saturating backbone + re-referenced refs is the correct, non-double-counting construction,
and the refs now help. But closed-shell exchange is capped ~75 (backbone reffree) to ~88 (referenced,

FEATURE SPACE (the SIMPLE l=0 monopole + l=1 s^2 do not fully determine the hole shape, so exact-hole
interpolation has irreducible residual) plus R_c=6 leakage -- NOT the backbone or the kernel tail.
Breaking it requires MORE FEATURES (tau / kinetic-energy density = the rSCAN ingredient; or higher
multipoles l=2+), not a better gradient enhancement. That is the next lever.
