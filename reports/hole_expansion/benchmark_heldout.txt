CONCLUSION: closed-shell (spin-consistent) references transfer BEST on both closed and open held-out atoms.
Spin-resolved references DEGRADE transfer and adding more doesn't help -- the spin-unpolarized functional
(input = total rho only, no spin) gets contradictory targets from spin-resolved holes. To use open-shell
spin-resolved refs, the functional would need spin-resolved INPUTS (spin densities / polarization feature).
=> Production stays on closed-shell references.
