Held-out benchmark: does adding the l=2 (quadrupole) axial feature help closed-shell references?
Same closed-shell refs + same FPS points (test atoms excluded); 10-dim [l0,l1] vs 11-dim [l0,l1,l2].
Eval = valence-only int(rho*eps_x) vs spin-resolved exact (atom_Emm), N=256 refs.

closed refs: 256 pts; built 10-dim (l0+l1) and 11-dim (l0+l1+l2)

MAE closed: closed 99  closed+l2(.5) 89  closed+l2(1) 94
MAE  open: closed 118  closed+l2(.5) 120  closed+l2(1) 119

VERDICT: l=2 gives a SMALL improvement on closed held-out (MAE 99->89 mHa at ell2=0.5, ~10%) and
NEGLIGIBLE change on open held-out (118->120). Consistent with l0+l1 already resolving the holes
smoothly (target Lipschitz ~0.02). l=2 is a minor refinement, not a structural gain. Implemented as a
flag (use_l2, default OFF) so production closed-shell functional is unchanged.
