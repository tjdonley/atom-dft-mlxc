ADDITIONAL SMOOTHING FOR SELF-CONSISTENT CONVERGENCE
=====================================================
Goal (user): "add the additional smoothing to get everything converging nicely.
Ensure that this doesn't have a significant impact on the energies."

CONTEXT
-------
The reference-mapped hole converges tightly as a smooth BACKBONE (LDA+Fermi-Amaldi,
no references: lambda=0 in the homotopy ramp -> residual ~5e-8). Turning the exact-hole
references ON (which supply the atomic binding energy) injects v_x roughness that stalls
SCF at ~1e-3. grad_filter (the GEA-motivated low-pass on the SPECTRAL GRADIENT operator,
keeping low-n / slowly-varying exact) removes the GRADIENT-channel piece of that roughness
and is energy-neutral, but the residual floor is MULTI-CHANNEL: the reference interpolation
is also rough in the monopole (cn) channels that feed the op_n^T term of v_x.

THE ADDITIONAL SMOOTHING (retargeted deriv_smooth)
--------------------------------------------------
deriv_smooth (min-roughness constrained shape fit): among coefficients that match the
references at the nodes (-> energy preserved), minimize the feature-derivative roughness
sum_d ||d sigma/d x_d||^2 (-> smoother v_x). The GEA c_G slope is re-pinned (two solves) so
the 10/81 limit is preserved.

KEY FIX: deriv_smooth_grad now targets the cn[1:]+s^2 ENERGY-RELEVANT channels (dims
0..n_out-1) -- the channels that feed the spatial-operator-amplified v_x terms (op_n^T
monopole + grad_op^T gradient) -- EXCLUDING the p2/Q completeness coordinates whose tight
length scales (w~50) otherwise dominate the Gram and smooth the wrong direction.
(Previously deriv_smooth_grad was s^2-only, dim [n_out-1].)

WHAT DIDN'T WORK
----------------
* More-aggressive grad_filter (gf=0.45 instead of 0.6): Ne DIVERGES at lambda=0.3
  (residual 7e2 -> 3e6) and biases the energy (Etot -32.25 vs -32.48). Over-smoothing the
  gradient operator distorts the gradient channel itself. Wrong lever.
* The v_x RANGE is a misleading proxy: deriv_smooth(cn+s^2) makes Ne's v_x min deeper
  (-10 -> -17) yet converges FAR better -- a smooth deep potential beats a spiky shallow one.

ELEGANT GENERALIZATION: deriv_smooth_adaptive (operator-amplification channel weights)
=======================================================================================
The deriv_smooth_grad dim-mask (hand-excluding p2/Q via dims=range(n_out)) is replaced by a
DERIVED, adaptive per-channel weight alpha_d = the v_x SPATIAL-OPERATOR amplification of channel
d (the factor that turns feature-derivative roughness into v_x roughness).

WHY operator amplification, NOT feature distance (the key insight): a purely feature-distance
metric CANNOT target the spiking channels. The roughness Gram weights each channel by ~(1/ell_d)^4,
and the energy kernel DELIBERATELY uses tiny ell for p2/Q (0.02, 0.3) to separate atoms in those
completeness coords -> the fitted sigma genuinely wiggles fast along p2/Q -> any feature-distance
penalty smooths p2/Q (which do NOT spike). What distinguishes the spiking channels is the SPATIAL
OPERATOR that carries each feature-derivative into v_x:
    v_x = eps + rho d eps/d rho + sum_n op_n^T(ew rho d eps/d c_n)/ew + grad_op^T(ew rho d eps/d g)/ew
Only cn (op_n^T) and s^2 (grad_op^T) have spatial operators; p2/Q enter via the LOCAL rho-term
(no spatial op) -> they cannot spike. So alpha_d = ||op_n|| (cn block), ||grad_op|| (s^2), 0 (p2/Q).
p2/Q are smoothed OUT of the penalty AUTOMATICALLY (alpha=0) -- the mask is DERIVED from the
operators, not hand-set, and robust if new features are added.

Operator spectral norms (Mg grid, gf=0.6): grad_op 87.6, op_n 76-229 -- all COMPARABLE (the GEA-
motivated grad_filter brought grad_op down to the op_n range), so the derived weights are nearly
democratic over cn+s^2: alpha normalized -> cn 1.02, s^2 0.85, p2 0, Q 0.

VALIDATION (gf=0.6 + deriv_smooth=1 + homotopy):
* Energy: IDENTICAL to the mask (closed-shell MAE 12.6 vs 12.6 mHa; Ex matches to 4 decimals --
  node-matching preserves the atomic energy regardless of the between-node reweighting).
* SCF (lambda=1 residual): adaptive Ne 2e-5 (mask 4e-5), Mg 6e-5 (mask 2e-4) -- comparable, slightly
  TIGHTER (the operator-informed, grid-adaptive cn/s^2 balance).
* Production confirm (scf_tol=3e-4): Ne/Mg/Ar/Kr all converged=True (see table at end).
* 14/14 FP tests pass (default-off; the deriv_smooth_grad mask path is bit-identical -- alpha=1 on
  dims 0..n_out-1, 0 elsewhere = the old loop).

deriv_smooth_adaptive is the RECOMMENDED production setting (elegant, mask-free, grid-adaptive,
energy-neutral, marginally better SCF). deriv_smooth_grad retained as the explicit predecessor.
