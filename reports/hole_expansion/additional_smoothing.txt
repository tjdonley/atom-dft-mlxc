ADDITIONAL SMOOTHING FOR SELF-CONSISTENT CONVERGENCE
=====================================================
Goal (user): "add the additional smoothing to get everything converging nicely.
Ensure that this doesn't have a significant impact on the energies."

CONTEXT
-------
The reference-mapped hole converges tightly as a smooth BACKBONE (LDA+Fermi-Amaldi,
no references: lambda=0 in the homotopy ramp -> residual ~5e-8). Turning the exact-hole
references ON (which supply the atomic binding energy) injects v_x roughness that stalls
SCF at ~1e-3. grad_filter (the GEA-motivated low-pass on the SPECTRAL GRADIENT operator,
keeping low-n / slowly-varying exact) removes the GRADIENT-channel piece of that roughness
and is energy-neutral, but the residual floor is MULTI-CHANNEL: the reference interpolation
is also rough in the monopole (cn) channels that feed the op_n^T term of v_x.

THE ADDITIONAL SMOOTHING (retargeted deriv_smooth)
--------------------------------------------------
deriv_smooth (min-roughness constrained shape fit): among coefficients that match the
references at the nodes (-> energy preserved), minimize the feature-derivative roughness
sum_d ||d sigma/d x_d||^2 (-> smoother v_x). The GEA c_G slope is re-pinned (two solves) so
the 10/81 limit is preserved.

KEY FIX: deriv_smooth_grad now targets the cn[1:]+s^2 ENERGY-RELEVANT channels (dims
0..n_out-1) -- the channels that feed the spatial-operator-amplified v_x terms (op_n^T
monopole + grad_op^T gradient) -- EXCLUDING the p2/Q completeness coordinates whose tight
length scales (w~50) otherwise dominate the Gram and smooth the wrong direction.
(Previously deriv_smooth_grad was s^2-only, dim [n_out-1].)

WHAT DIDN'T WORK
----------------
* More-aggressive grad_filter (gf=0.45 instead of 0.6): Ne DIVERGES at lambda=0.3
  (residual 7e2 -> 3e6) and biases the energy (Etot -32.25 vs -32.48). Over-smoothing the
  gradient operator distorts the gradient channel itself. Wrong lever.
* The v_x RANGE is a misleading proxy: deriv_smooth(cn+s^2) makes Ne's v_x min deeper
  (-10 -> -17) yet converges FAR better -- a smooth deep potential beats a spiky shallow one.
