(scale-free) = energy spread among the closest 25% cross-atom feature matches (lower = lower floor;
reference: in-domain floor ~0.0008 scale-free ~ 1 mHa). Bispectrum = per-n-diagonal axial CG triples.

KEY CONCLUSIONS:
1. Lowest floor across ALL atoms: Vfull signed l<=6 (0.001 ~ 1 mHa) or pow+bisp l<=4 (0.0017). For
   atoms the SIGNED axial coefficients are valid invariants (the radial axis is canonical) and most
   efficient; the rotation-invariant bispectrum is the route if no canonical frame (molecules).
2. The current cn+s2 is cross-atom INCOMPLETE: closed-shell needs +l2pow; open-shell needs the full
   4-body (bispectrum) / signed-vector information.
3. NO 1-RDM energy wall: the full density descriptor reaches ~1 mHa even on open-shell, so the gap is
   descriptor INCOMPLETENESS, not missing spin/orbital (1-RDM) information.
4. The cross-atom floor is REACHED (close matches transfer) -- so a functional using the complete
   invariant set + enough references can in principle transfer across the periodic table at ~1 mHa.
