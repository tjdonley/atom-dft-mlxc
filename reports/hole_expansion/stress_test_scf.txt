Convergence is the signal. CAVEAT: the heavy-atom energy columns are UNRELIABLE -- not density drift
(the SCF density barely changes vs HF and the functional's own int(rho*eps_x) is sensible, e.g. Ar -3.06),
but a SOLVER bug: energy_components.exchange disagrees with int(rho*eps_x) by up to ~1.6 Ha for Ar+ while
matching exactly for light atoms (Ne). Use int(rho*eps_x) directly for energies; trust only the conv column.
